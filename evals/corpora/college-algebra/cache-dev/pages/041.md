466 CHAPTER 6 Exponential and ­Logarithmic Functions exponential function For any real number x, an exponential function is a function with the form f(x) = abx where •	 a is the a non-zero real number called the initial value and •	 b is any positive real number such that b ≠ 1. •	 The domain of f is all real numbers. •	 The range of f is all positive real numbers if a > 0. •	 The range of f is all negative real numbers if a < 0. •	 Th y-intercept is (0, a), and the horizontal asymptote is y = 0.
